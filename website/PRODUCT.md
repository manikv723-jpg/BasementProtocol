# Basement Protocol
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Companies and Indian SMEs evaluating custom AI systems; individuals exploring workflow tools. The guides section serves the Manikk.ai audience looking for practical explanations of builds shown in content.

## Product Purpose
Showcase enterprise AI services, company tools and individual templates. Publish the owner's Manikk.ai guides on the same domain to attract relevant readers and connect useful learning to the business.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js website with existing payment and enquiry flows. Guides are readable public articles with original PDF downloads. Only supplied, reviewed material is published. Measured results are attributed to the guide, not promoted as independent benchmarks or guaranteed outcomes. No invented downloadable app or public source repository.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Basement Protocol's established dark visual identity and current site functionality. Credit Manikk.ai as the guide author.

## Evidence on Hand
The owner supplied SayOpen_Jev_Guide.pdf on 27 September 2026 as the first guide. Four pages covering the voice workflow, tests, implementation, bugs and limitations. Public availability of SayOpen is not established; the PDF states it is not a public download.

## Product Principles
- Teach the real workflow, including its limitations.
- Keep articles usable without downloading a PDF or providing an email.
- Connect relevant reading to services without interrupting it.
